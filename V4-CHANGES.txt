Deriv AI Analyzer V4 Focused Cleanup

- Master entry engine now produces TOUCH / NO TOUCH only.
- Rise/Fall is retained as market-bias context, not a competing master signal.
- Entry requires more samples, evidence threshold, dominance, travel and persistence confirmation.

- Touch/No Touch percentages are presented as evidence scores rather than certainty.
- Chart height and workspace spacing were tightened for laptop screens.
- Sidebar was grouped into Workspace, Market tools, Automation and System.
