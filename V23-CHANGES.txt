V23 - Independent Above/Below + Timeframe Warm-Up

- Above Spot and Below Spot remain visible side-by-side.
- Each barrier side is analyzed independently with its own higher-timeframe context score.
- Timed chart intervals no longer publish an entry until enough completed candles exist.
  * 1m requires at least 30 completed candles.
  * 5s/15s/30s require at least 36 completed candles.
  * Tick mode requires at least 120 ticks.
- When data is insufficient, both sides show WARMING UP and Master Entry remains WAIT.
- Increased analyzer page seed from 500 to 6500 available Deriv ticks so 1m history can actually populate.
- Higher-timeframe context now gates raw Touch/No Touch candidates; context conflicts force WAIT.
- Chart footer shows READY or warming progress for the selected timeframe.
- Existing compact one-screen layout, colors and fixed side-by-side scanner are preserved.
